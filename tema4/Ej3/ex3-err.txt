Errors and Corrections for ex3.xml

1. Tag Nesting Error:
   - Incorrect: <president>Bill Gates <extension>09-9990011</president></extension>
   - Incorrect: <secretary>Katharine Finch <extension>09-9990012</secretary></extension>
   - Incorrect: <president>Larry Ellison <extension>09-8880011</president></extension>
   - Incorrect: <secretary>Helen Calhoun <extension>09-8880012</secretary></extension>
   - Correction: <extension> must be a child element and closed before the parent element closes.
   - Example Correction:
     <president>Bill Gates<extension>09-9990011</extension></president>
     <secretary>Katharine Finch<extension>09-9990012</extension></secretary>
     <president>Larry Ellison<extension>09-8880011</extension></president>
     <secretary>Helen Calhoun<extension>09-8880012</extension></secretary>

2. Logical Structure Suggestion (Not a syntax error):
   - Multiple company, president, and secretary elements are allowed, but grouping them under each company is recommended for clarity.
